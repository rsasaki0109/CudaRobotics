# CudaNav crossing pedestrians: dynamic-obstacle MPPI

Generated by `scripts/render_cudanav_dynamic_obstacles.py` from 30 runs of `cudarobotics-cudanav sim --scenario crossing`.
Synthetic S-course, one traversal, two pedestrians crossing the corridors;
the tracker is a simulator oracle except in the scan-tracker condition. See `docs/cudanav_dynamic_obstacles.md`.

Tracking accuracy against the true pedestrians (visible = hit by at least 3 rays; tracked = a confirmed track within 0.5 m; false = a confirmed track more than 0.7 m from every pedestrian):

| Condition | Recall | Median position RMSE | Median velocity RMSE | False tracks per frame |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scan tracker (no oracle) + CV prediction | 90.5% | 0.12 m | 0.28 m/s | 0.72 |

- Goal: runs that reached the goal within 60 s (600 frames).
- Robot-caused collisions: wall contacts plus moves that deepen a pedestrian contact.
- Pedestrian yields: pedestrian-frames spent paused because the robot was in the way.
- Non-wall cells: occupied cells more than 0.15 m from every wall (leftover traces plus pedestrians currently in the map).
